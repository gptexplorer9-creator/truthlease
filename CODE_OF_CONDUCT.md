# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

TruthLease is committed to a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone, regardless of experience, background, or identity. Contributing should be a harassment-free experience.

## Our standards

Helpful behavior includes being respectful, patient, and welcoming; giving and accepting constructive feedback; and focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal or political attacks, publishing another person's private information without permission, and conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and commits, and when representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's **Security > Report a vulnerability** flow or contact [@gptexplorer9-creator](https://github.com/gptexplorer9-creator). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may take appropriate action, up to a temporary or permanent ban. For serious abuse, use GitHub's [report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) flow.
